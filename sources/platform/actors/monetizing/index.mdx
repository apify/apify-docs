You can monetize your automation, AI agent, and web scraping projects by publishing them as paid Actors on [Apify Store](https://apify.com/store).

## Pricing models

You can publish Actors on Apify Store under one of the following pricing models:

- [Pay per event (PPE)](/actors/monetize/pay-per-event): Users pay for specific events that are programmatically triggered from the Actor's source code. You can define these events and include actions such as generating a single result or starting an Actor. You can also choose whether to pass the platform usage costs on to users.
- Pay per usage: Users can run the Actor without any additional charges beyond the platform usage costs generated by the Actor.

For a detailed comparison of pricing models from the perspective of your users, see [Actors in Store](/actors/running/actors-in-store).

## Fees and costs

Your payout is your Actor's revenue minus Apify's commission and platform usage costs. For details, see [Pricing and costs](/actors/monetize/pricing-and-costs).

### Platform usage

Running Actors generates platform usage costs for infrastructure, proxies, and storage. Who covers these costs depends on the pricing model and the mode in which the Actor runs.

For pay-per-event Actors, Apify deducts platform usage costs from your payout. If your Actor pricing doesn't cover these costs, you might run into a [negative profit](/actors/monetize/pay-per-event#avoid-negative-profit). However, you'll never have to pay Apify for other users running your Actors.

### Testing costs

When you test your Actor on the Apify platform, costs for running the Actor are deducted from your prepaid usage.

:::tip Try Creator plan

With the [Creator plan](https://apify.com/pricing/creator-plan), you get $500 of prepaid usage to test your Actors during the first 6 months.

:::

### Apify's commission

Apify keeps 20% of your Actor's revenue as a commission. Apify will never charge you if your Actor doesn't earn anything.

## Make your Actor eligible for agentic payments

Agentic payments let AI agents run and pay for your Actor without an Apify account. Which Actors an agent can run depends on how it pays:

- Agents that buy a prepaid token from [Apify AGI](/agents/payments) over [x402](/integrations/x402) or [MPP](/integrations/mpp) can run any Actor that uses [limited permissions](/actors/development/permissions). The pricing model doesn't matter.
- Agents that pay per request, such as with [Skyfire](/integrations/skyfire), can run only Actors that meet the requirements below.

## Handle free users

When monetizing your Actor, you might want to limit features or usage for users on the Apify free plan. If you choose to do this, you _must_ handle it transparently:

- Communicate upfront: Clearly state any limitations in your Actor's `README` and input schema. Users should know about restrictions _before_ they run the Actor.
- Graceful exits: If a free user hits a limit, don't crash the Actor or return a system error. Instead, exit gracefully with a clear [status message](/actors/development/programming-interface/status-messages#communicating-limitations) explaining the limit (e.g., "Free tier limit reached").
- Avoid confusion: Never make a policy restriction look like a bug or platform error.

## Actor analytics

Monitor your Actors' performance through the [Actor Analytics](https://console.apify.com/actors/insights/analytics) dashboard under **Development > Insights > Analytics**.

The analytics dashboard allows you to select specific Actors and view key metrics aggregated across all user runs:

- Revenue, costs and profit trends over time
- User growth metrics (both paid and free users)
- Cost per 1,000 results to optimize pricing
- Run success rate statistics
- User acquisition funnel analytics
- Shared debug runs from users

All metrics can be exported as JSON for custom analysis and reporting.
