Publish Actors on Apify Store to turn your web scraping and automation code into products that earn money, without the extra work of building a traditional SaaS.

The process involves the following steps:

1. [Development](/actors/development). Build your Actor using [Apify SDKs](https://docs.apify.com/sdk), [Crawlee](https://crawlee.dev/), or [Actor templates](https://apify.com/templates).
1. [Testing](/actors/development/test-and-debug). Ensure that your Actor works reliably with automated or manual tests.
1. [Publication](/actors/publishing/publish) and [monetization](/actors/monetize). Make your Actor public in Apify Store and choose a pricing model.
1. [Promotion](/actors/promote). Optimize for search engines, share your Actor on social media, and create tutorials.

## Benefits of publishing your Actor

When you publish your Actor on Apify Store, you eliminate the complexity of building and maintaining a traditional SaaS product. The Apify platform handles infrastructure, billing, and distribution, so you can focus on your code.

### Skip the infrastructure work

Apify handles the infrastructure around your Actor:

- Apify's cloud runs your Actor and scales it automatically.
- Apify supports multiple payment methods and automates billing and transactions.
- Your Actor's page in Apify Store replaces a domain and website of your own.

### Reach users

Apify Store puts your Actor in front of people who are already looking for automation tools:

- Your Actor gets its own page in Apify Store, which users find through search and categories.
- Users can run your Actor through the API, schedule it, monitor it, and connect it to integrations. Apify provides these tools.
- Built-in analytics show how people use your Actor. Use these insights to improve your Actors and optimize pricing.

### Generate passive income

When you publish and actively promote your Actor, it can generate recurring revenue with minimal maintenance.

Read the success stories of developers who have monetized their Actors on Apify Store:

- [Caleb David (microworlds)](https://blog.apify.com/web-scraping-freelance-financial-freedom/) achieved financial freedom through Actor development.
- [Tugkan (epctex)](https://apify.com/success-stories/paid-actor-journey-apify-freelancer-tugkan) built a successful freelance career with paid Actors.

### Build your portfolio

A public Actor demonstrates your skills publicly.

Your Actors are examples of your work that can build your reputation in the developer community. This visibility can open freelance opportunities and establish you as a subject matter expert.

## Maintain public Actors

Public Actors require a higher level of responsibility than private ones.

Once your Actor is available in [Apify Store](https://apify.com/store), other people build their work on top of it. Plan to spend about 2 hours a week on maintenance:

- Addressing bug reports and issues
- Implementing updates and improvements
- Responding to user questions and feedback

### Handle breaking changes

Keep improving your Actor, but watch for changes that can break the integrations that users have already built. Always release a breaking change as a new version. For details, see [Version public Actors](/actors/development/version-public-actors).

### Documentation and testing

Pay special attention to your Actor's documentation. Write your [README](/actors/publishing/actor-readme) in plain language and avoid jargon, because many of your users aren't developers.

Test your Actor regularly, either manually or with [automated tests](/actors/development/test-and-debug) and [monitoring](https://apify.com/apify/monitoring), so you find problems before your users do.

## Compete with other Actors

Apify Store allows for competition between Actors. You can publish a scraper for a specific website even if similar Actors are already available.

However, Apify penalizes copyright infringement. If someone copies elements of your Actor, [contact Apify support](https://apify.com/contact).
